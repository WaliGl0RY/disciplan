<a name="study-docs"></a>
<p><a href="#study-docs"><img src="images/ep-zone-docs.svg" alt="Study documents" width="100%"></a></p>

Each study document is study material, not a reference book: nothing is assumed that isn't explained in it. Always the same six parts: **1 Orientierung** · **2 Grundlagen** (every term from zero, intuition before formalism, self-check questions) · **3 Verfahren** (procedures, each with *why* it works) · **4 Übungen** (solutions hidden in `<details>`) · **5 Klausurtaktik** · **6 Quellen zum Üben**. <a name="trainers"></a>
<p><a href="#trainers"><img src="images/ep-zone-trainers.svg" alt="Trainers" width="100%"></a></p>

Five self-test apps, one per module that needed one. Each is a single HTML file. After answering, every option is judged with a reason, followed by an explanation, the source, and a link back into the study document. Pick one to see its main screen and a round:

<a name="trainer-bwr"></a>
<details>
<summary><a href="#trainer-bwr"><img src="images/pill-trainer-bwr.svg" alt="BWR trainer" height="28"></a> &nbsp;<sub>5 modes · 304 vocabulary cards · 104 lecture tasks · progress saved in the browser</sub></summary>
<br>
<p align="center"><a href="#trainers"><img src="images/trainer-bwr-home.png" alt="BWR trainer main screen" width="700"></a></p>
<p align="center"><a href="#trainers"><img src="images/trainer-bwr-round.gif" alt="BWR trainer round" width="600"></a></p>

**Shown: vocabulary cards.** A term or its definition from the lecture, typed from memory, in both directions. Then the model answer, an English gloss and, where it helps, the *Wortfamilie*. You rate yourself (*Hatte ich · Teilweise · Nicht gewusst*), and the weak ones come back.
</details>

<a name="trainer-its"></a>
<details>
<summary><a href="#trainer-its"><img src="images/pill-trainer-its.svg" alt="ITS trainer" height="28"></a> &nbsp;<sub>3 modes · 14 lecture quizzes · 255 questions, 39 with generators · progress saved in the browser</sub></summary>
<br>
<p align="center"><a href="#trainers"><img src="images/trainer-its-home.png" alt="ITS trainer main screen" width="700"></a></p>
<p align="center"><a href="#trainers"><img src="images/trainer-its-round.gif" alt="ITS trainer round" width="600"></a></p>

**Shown: a lecture quiz, one question at a time.** Calculation questions are generators: after a correct answer, *„Mit neuen Zahlen üben“* builds the same question with fresh numbers, so you practise the procedure, not the answer. Quizzes also run as a sheet (5, 10 or all questions per page).
</details>

<a name="trainer-pi2"></a>
<details>
<summary><a href="#trainer-pi2"><img src="images/pill-trainer-pi2.svg" alt="PI2 trainer" height="28"></a> &nbsp;<sub>6 modes · 88 chapter questions in 7 chapters · progress saved in the browser</sub></summary>
<br>
<p align="center"><a href="#trainers"><img src="images/trainer-pi2-home.png" alt="PI2 trainer main screen" width="700"></a></p>
<p align="center"><a href="#trainers"><img src="images/trainer-pi2-round.gif" alt="PI2 trainer round" width="600"></a></p>

**Shown: chapter questions, one at a time.** Each question comes from a script chapter (10–17). Wrong options explain *why* they are wrong, so a correct click still teaches the neighbouring cases.
</details>

<a name="trainer-gsp"></a>
<details>
<summary><a href="#trainer-gsp"><img src="images/pill-trainer-gsp.svg" alt="GSP trainer" height="28"></a> &nbsp;<sub>1 mode · 69 questions in 6 chapters · a primer per chapter</sub></summary>
<br>
<p align="center"><a href="#trainers"><img src="images/trainer-gsp-home.png" alt="GSP trainer main screen" width="520"></a></p>
<p align="center"><a href="#trainers"><img src="images/trainer-gsp-round.gif" alt="GSP trainer round" width="520"></a></p>

**Shown: chapter A.** Every chapter opens with a *„Kurz erklärt“* primer (bit operators, nibbles, masks, shifts …), then the questions, with one sentence of feedback after each check.
</details>

<a name="trainer-bvs2"></a>
<details>
<summary><a href="#trainer-bvs2"><img src="images/pill-trainer-bvs2.svg" alt="BVS2 trainer" height="28"></a> &nbsp;<sub>9 lectures · 501 lecture questions · 101 summary questions · progress saved in the browser</sub></summary>
<br>
<p align="center"><a href="#trainers"><img src="images/trainer-bvs2-home.png" alt="BVS2 trainer main screen" width="700"></a></p>
<p align="center"><a href="#trainers"><img src="images/trainer-bvs2-round.gif" alt="BVS2 trainer round" width="700"></a></p>

The main screen lists the nine lectures with their state, a summary-question mix per lecture, and practice rounds. Each lecture has its script and a question round. The ✓ on a question opens the correction next to it: every option with its reason and the slide it comes from. The ? shows how the same point could be asked differently.
</details>
